Every file below was downloaded from the public Plumbing 101, LLC website, https://www.plumbing101llc.com/. Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, and Google Business photos could not be retrieved without a login, so none of those files are in this repo.

The shield logo is the only company image shown on the pages. The other four files are stored here because they are on the company site. Their file names and EXIF titles match generic stock photos, including a Wix section titled Inventory Management, so they are not presented as Plumbing 101 jobs, crew, or trucks.
