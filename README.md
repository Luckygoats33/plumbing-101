# Plumbing 101, LLC

Static website for Plumbing 101, LLC, a Lincoln City plumbing company that serves Lincoln County and Tillamook County, Oregon. The pages are plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript for GitHub Pages.

Canonical site: https://luckygoats33.github.io/plumbing-101/

Open `index.html` in a browser, or from this folder run:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

Then visit http://127.0.0.1:8000/

## Left out because it could not be verified, or because sources conflict

- Street address. Google-style directories often say 3003 SE Hwy 101, Lincoln City, OR 97367. A business-registry mirror says 3003 SW Highway 101 and also lists older addresses (2925 NW Port Ave, 153 S Wells Dr, 1220 SE Oar Ave). The company website and the BBB overview do not print a street. OpenStreetMap ties 3003 Oregon Coast Highway to a flooring shop at nearly the same coordinates directories use for this plumber. No street number is published here.
- Google star rating and review count. Public directories show 4.8 with counts that do not match (about 33 to 36). The Google profile itself could not be read directly, and review text from those directories is not republished.
- Map-listing hours of 8:30 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. The site uses 3:30 p.m., which matches the company website and the BBB profile.
- Bond amount, insurance amount, BCD license PB2405, employee count, and workers' compensation details. Those appear on third-party contractor pages, not on the official CCB summary that was retrieved.
- Responsible managing individual title. Third-party CCB mirrors name Michael Elliot Worthington. The official summary page that loaded did not print the RMI. The site uses the BBB titles only.
- Personal or agent addresses from registry mirrors. Not used as a shop location.
- "Inventory Management" text on the company homepage. It reads as unused template copy, so it is not listed as a service.
- After-hours or 24-hour service. Not stated on the company site or the BBB hours table.
- Additional testimonials, years of personal experience, staff names, certifications beyond the CCB license and BBB accreditation, and photos of jobs. Not invented.
