Drains

A leak under the sink trap

Loose slip nuts, cracked plastic, and thin metal traps.

The common drip

Plastic slip nuts loosen. A quarter turn by hand, with the sink empty, sometimes stops a weep. A cracked washer, a tailpiece bottomed out in the trap, or a cross-threaded nut will not seal. Forcing plastic splits it.

Water only when the dishwasher runs may be the hose, not the trap. Water only when the sprayer is used is the sprayer. Have someone run each thing while you watch.

Old metal

Chrome traps can look fine while the metal is paper thin. If it flexes, it is due. Put a bucket under it before you loosen anything. If a chemical opener was used in the last day, wear gloves or leave the trap for the plumber.

A photo under the sink shows which parts to bring. Drain and sink service includes this.

Safe checks

  • Tighten plastic nuts by hand.
  • Test the dishwasher and sprayer separately.
  • Bucket a rusty metal trap.
  • Warn about chemical opener.
