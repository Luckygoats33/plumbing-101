Coastal homes

Opening a beach house

A walk-through before anyone uses a house that has been closed.

Pressure, then listen

Open the main slowly. Walk the lowest fixtures and the water heater. With every faucet off, listen. A toilet that refills has a leaking flapper or a stuck fill valve. A hiss in a wall is a reason to shut the main again and call.

Fill traps that may have dried: showers, floor drains, and the laundry standpipe. Run a little hot water and look at the heater fittings. Supply lines that sat all winter sometimes fail on the first pressurization. Look under each sink.

Before guests

A running toilet can run for days in a rental before anyone reports it. A short walk now is the cheaper option. If something failed and the leak is stopped, email office@plumbing101.net with photos. For a live leak, call (541) 614-1344 on a weekday between 8:30 a.m. and 3:30 p.m.

Residential plumbing, toilets, faucets, drains, and water heaters are all on the published list.

Safe checks

  • Open the main slowly.
  • Listen with fixtures off.
  • Refill dry traps.
  • Check under sinks and at the heater.
