Remodels

Questions before a bathroom remodel

Plumbing decisions to settle before a bathroom is opened up.

Same place or a new place

A new faucet and toilet in the same spots is a different project from moving a shower to another wall. Moving a toilet means moving the flange and the vent relationship. Moving a shower means a new trap and a valve at the right height. Bathroom installation and remodel work is on the BBB list.

Ask who opens the wall and who closes it. Ask whether old shutoffs will be replaced while they are exposed. Measure the door swing before a larger toilet is chosen.

Added baths in old cottages

Many coast cottages have a bath that was once a porch. Drains may run flat and vents may be small. A slow drain should be discussed before tile goes up. Pipe inspection before demo belongs in the estimate if the drains have already been a problem.

Ask how the permit and inspection are handled. Keep the written scope.

Safe checks

  • Decide which fixtures move.
  • Ask who patches and paints.
  • Replace tired shutoffs while the wall is open.
  • Ask about the permit.
