Tillamook County

Plumbing in Tillamook, Oregon

Plumbing 101, LLC states that it serves Lincoln County and Tillamook County. Tillamook is in Tillamook County. The company is based in Lincoln City and posts weekday hours of 8:30 a.m. to 3:30 p.m.

The town

Tillamook is the county seat, set in a broad dairy valley that takes heavy rain. The town is inland from the beaches, with river flood history in the surrounding valley.

Houses and buildings

Housing runs from older in-town homes to ranch houses on the valley floor. High groundwater after storms can wet crawlspaces and confuse a leak hunt.

What to mention when you call

Say whether the water showed up only after rainfall, and whether it is at a fixture or under the house. A water heater in a garage is common. Mention if that space got wet.
